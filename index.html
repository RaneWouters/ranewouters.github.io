Hello! I am a Ph.D. student at China University of Geosciences, advised by Prof. Chi Xu and Prof. Li Cheng.

My research focuses on reliable visual perception and estimation for imperfect observations and physical interaction. Currently, I am working on:

  • (3D Vision) Reconstructing and understanding geometric structure from images.
  • (Pose Estimation) Estimating reliable camera and object poses under challenging conditions.
  • (Hand-Object Interaction) Understanding contact, force, and interaction between hands and objects.

Open to collaborations and conversations. Let's talk!
